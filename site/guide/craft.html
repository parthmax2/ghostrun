Prompt Crafting & Optimization (`ghostrun craft`)

Stop hand-tuning prompts by trial and error. Declare what you want as a typed signature ("inputs -> outputs"), provide a few examples, and let GhostRun's Bayesian optimizer discover the highest-performing instructions and few-shot demonstrations automatically.

Why Hand-Tuning Prompts Fails

When prompts break, developers usually tweak random words in system instructions and hope it fixes edge cases without breaking existing tests. This creates brittle, fragile prompts.

GhostRun Craft treats prompt engineering as a compiler problem: it runs automated Bayesian search trials across candidate instructions and selects the combinations that maximize holdout test accuracy.

1. The 10-Second Quickstart

Define a Typed Signature

Signatures declare your input and output fields cleanly:

from ghostrun.craft import craft

# Optimize instructions and few-shot demonstrations automatically
crafted = craft(
    name="refund_classifier",
    signature="customer_message -> is_refund_request: bool, urgency: int",
    examples_path="dataset/support_queries.jsonl",
    criterion="Accurately flags refund requests and evaluates urgency from 1 to 5",
    model="gpt-4o-mini",
    budget=10,  # Bayesian search iterations
)

print("🏆 Winning Instructions:\n", crafted.instructions)
print("🎯 Holdout Accuracy Score:", crafted.holdout_score)

2. Using the CLI

You can also craft and optimize prompts directly from your terminal:

ghostrun craft triage_bot \
    --signature "ticket_text -> priority, department" \
    --examples dataset.jsonl \
    --criterion "Correctly classifies urgent server outages to DevOps" \
    --model gpt-4o-mini \
    --budget 10

The resulting winning prompt is saved locally into .ghostrun_cache/prompts/ and is immediately ready to replay in your pytest suite for $0 with zero network latency.
